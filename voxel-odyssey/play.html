VOXEL ODYSSEY

Pick how hard the game should work. You can change it any time in Settings → Performance.

▶ Play — automatic Looks at your computer and picks the graphics for it. Start here. ▶ Play — older laptop Low preset: shorter view distance, simpler lighting, lower resolution. ▶ Play — very slow computer Smallest picture, shortest view, capped at 30 frames a second. Plays on almost anything with WebGL 2.

If it runs badly

  • Close other tabs and programs; use a current Chrome, Edge or Firefox.
  • Turn on hardware acceleration in the browser's settings (it is sometimes off on managed machines).
  • Press F3 in game for the frame-time profiler.
  • No admin rights? Nothing here installs anything. A portable browser on a USB stick works too.

Keep these together

  • play.html and the dist folder (with voxel-odyssey.html inside). Or skip this page and just open dist/voxel-odyssey.html.
